# The World
The world of this campaign is known as the Great Plane, an expanse of sea and land that spans the entire universe. The campaign takes place on a few of the dozens of continents on the surface of the Great Plane.

More specifically, the campaign prominently features the Academy of Garreg Mach, a prostegious academy for training those gifted with powers and strengths few others are. This is where the party will start their adventure. The Academy exists inside a prominent city-state of the same name on the continent of Valm. Valm is made up of a number of vassal-countries that exist under the thumb of a far-away and mysterious Imperium, and Garreg Mach is neutral where these countries have done diplomacy for centuries before the Imperium's arrival. Alongside the Academy is a prominent pantheonic church which houses a large orphanage.

The Academy is where many noble families send their children to learn both the art of diplomacy and the strength needed to survive in this tumultuos political climate. However, not everyone in the Academy holds noble backgrounds. The Academy seeks out special individuals from across the continent. Its enigmatic headmaster, Robin Salaman, sees to it that no diamond is ever left in the rough - whether they like it our not.

# The Party
The party are all first-year students to the academy. They can be entirely unfamiliar witht the other members of the party, or have an extensive shared backstory; it is entirely up to the players. The Academy is somewhere between a highschool and a college, but there is no maximum age of entry. Students must be at least sixteen to enroll, but can be much older, though students entering above the age of 30 are very uncommon. Dorms are separated by age first, then by rank in the Academy.

# Character tips
## Alignment
Characters should be Neutral or Good on the morality alignment scale, but may be Chaotic, Lawful, or Neutral on the law scale.

## Classes
Generally, classes are unrestricted, however there are certain classes that may feel redundant. Over the course of this campaign, all party members will recieve dragon-themed abilities and/or obtain dragonic campanions. Class options that are dragon themed could go well with these abilities, or it may seem too similar. Similarly, Classes with animal companions or other helpful creatures one controls can be overwhelming, so it is reccomended to not choose such classes, such as the base Ranger or Summoner (though certain archetypes may work).

## Languages
Language works somewhat differently in this campaign as opposed to pure Pathfinder, as languages are usually driven by "race". Instead, languages are very much regional. Garreg Mach has two official languages: Cael and High Imperial. For the purposes of character building, Cael should be thought of as "common" for your characters and will be what is spoken the most, especially early in the campaign.

Thematically, if one cares, Cael sounds phonetically similar to Spanish while High Imperial sounds very much like english with specifically a british dialect.

## Species
Referred to as "races" in Pathfinder. There are no hard limits on what species a character is, but players should ask their GM about anything outside of what is core to Pathfinder.

## Religion
There are a number of deities worshipped throughout the continent. Ask your DM for particulars if you wish to build a character with a particular religious bend.

# Garreg Mach
Garreg Mach as a city and as an academy is the primary setting for the beginning of the campaign. Players take on the role of students at this academy as they figure out their powers and uncover the mysteries behind its founder and the looming Imperium, as well as other unseen dangers that lurk in the shadows.

## The Academy

## Downtime (Student Duties and clocks)
Players will have to attend classes as a downtime activity. Their progress in their classes are tracked as a "clock."

Downtime is time the players aren't actively playing the game. This is the amount of time that passes in-universe between sessions or between break periods within a session. This is time where the players can do a limited set of in-game actions that aren't really approriate to take time for during an actual session (for example, studying for a test or exercising.)

A "clock" is a visual tracker with multiple segements in the shape of a circle. They can represent any abstract goal any character or the party as a whole has, or even relationships or ongoing events. During downtime, players may take a number of downtime actions according to the GM. They will usually be asked to roll one or more skills. These rolls will determine how one or more of their clocks are affected. This will be given more time to be explained later.

At the very least, each player will have a clock for how well they're doing in their classes this semester. The goal should be to fill the clock. The student's grade will be determined by how filled the clock is at the end of the semester.

## Notable NPCs
### Robin Salaman (Human, Female) – Headmaster
Pragmatic but willful, this misterious human seemed to come from nowhere shortly after the Imperium conquered the continent. She was incredibly versed in the political climate of the continent and combined with the apparent sorceress's power, quickly rose to prominence. Initial suspicions were that the apparent sorceress was an imperial plant, however, she has squarely put herself in opposition of many of the Imperium's diplomatic maneuvering in the area, earning her the respect of many diplomats. Regardless of her intentions, she is an excellent teacher with an eye for talent, and a firebrand in the Academy's tradition of finding talent at all stratas of the social order.

### Jeof Landing (Human, Male) - Imperial Emissary
The head emissary and permanent resident of the Imperial embassy located in Garreg Mach, he is a domineering presence. For now, the Imperial army's presence is minimal as it is with many of its vassals, but Jeof is an expansionist and believes the entirety of the Great Plane is Imperial jurisdiction.

# Valm
Valm is the continent we'll be starting on in this campaign. Over the last couple centuries, it was invaded by the Imperium amidst a continental war and subjugated. The countries were somewhat reorganized and reestablished to suit the Imperium before the breadth of the Imperium's forces left, with regular tithes required from all subjugated parties. The countries now more or less go about "business as usual" with a tense understanding that if any of them step out of line, the boot of the Imperium might return heavier than before.

One of the tenser issues is the subject of worship. Under Imperium law, only one deity is recognized, a foreign entity known as Hewah. However, Robin Salaman in particular has risen in opposition to this. Her arguments have so far prevented the disbandment of the Pantheonic Church of Garreg Mach, much to the chagrin of the Imperial emmissaries who push for worship of Hewah across the continent.
